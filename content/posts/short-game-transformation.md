I want to tell you something that no one told me when I started playing seriously: the driver doesn't matter nearly as much as you think.

I spent the first two years of my golf obsession chasing distance. New driver. Swing lessons focused on power. YouTube deep-dives into how the tour pros generate 120+ mph clubhead speed. My handicap went from 24 to 19 and then sat there, immovable, for eleven months.

Then I spent a single hour with a short game coach named Marcus, and everything changed.

## What Marcus Showed Me

Marcus didn't have me hit a single chip for the first twenty minutes. He just made me watch — specifically, where my misses were landing on the practice green. Long. Always long. With no spin, no touch, no feel.

"You're using your arms," he said. "Golf's cruelest paradox: you get more distance from your short shots by using less."

The lesson that followed was about controlling acceleration. Not stopping at the ball, but accelerating *through* it with a narrow swing arc. The hands lead. The wrists stay firm. The ball pops up with backspin.

I shot 87 the next day. Then 86. Then 84.

## The Specific Changes

Here's what I actually changed, in plain language:

**Around the green:** I switched from my gap wedge for everything to selecting the right loft for the shot — 56° for standard chips, 60° only when I need height, 9-iron for bump-and-runs from tight lies.

**From the bunker:** Open the face. Open the stance. Hit two inches behind the ball. Finish high. I used to blade every third bunker shot. Now I get up and down from sand roughly 40% of the time, which isn't tour-level but it's no longer a disaster.

**Putting:** I switched to a cross-handed grip in February. My stroke went from handsy and inconsistent to smooth and repeatable within three weeks. My three-putt percentage dropped by more than half.

> The woods are full of long drivers. — Anonymous

## The Handicap Story

| Month | Handicap |
|-------|----------|
| October 2024 | 19.1 |
| November 2024 | 18.6 |
| December 2024 | 17.4 |
| February 2025 | 16.0 |
| April 2025 | 14.8 |

That's a 4.3-stroke drop in six months. Every single stroke came from within 80 yards.

I still miss fairways. I still find rough. My iron game is fine but not exceptional. But now when I make a mistake off the tee, I can scramble. And scrambling is what the game is actually about.
